## How to use this tracker

The tracker has 70 posts in 7 buckets, 10 per bucket. Each post has a target keyword, a writing style to model, a length and a status. Pick a status as a post moves along, and paste the draft link or editing notes in the last column. The keyword research behind it is in [Keyword Research — citations.agency Blog](https://claude.ai/artifact/QCCAqTc4g4GAnvmbAKmzuT).

**Status flow:** Idea → Outlining → Drafting → In review → Edits → Ready → Published.

**Launch posts** are marked ★. These are the first 12 to write, taken from both earlier plans.

## Writing styles to model

Most posts follow RedShip's two formats, because those formats rank across the Reddit searches we checked. Model the structure, never the wording. Three other formats cover the posts RedShip's formats don't fit.

| Style | Modelled on | Length | Structure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **RS Playbook** | [RedShip — Reddit marketing for SaaS playbook](https://redship.io/blog/reddit-marketing-saas-playbook) | ~2,000–2,500 words | Title with a colon and year: "X: the complete playbook (2026)". Author, updated date and read time on one line. **TL;DR box** with the thesis and timeline. A short, sharp intro that ends with who the guide is for. Sentence-case H2 statements, with phases carrying week ranges. 3–6 short paragraphs per section, bold lead-ins, 1–2 tables, a "Don't do this" callout. About 10 internal links. A 5-question FAQ near the end. A CTA box at the end plus one mention mid-article. |
| **RS Answer** | [RedShip Learn — Reddit marketing for SaaS](https://redship.io/learn/reddit-marketing-for-saas) | ~800–1,000 words | Keyword-first title. An 80–100 word **Quick Answer** block, then 5 **Key Takeaways** bullets. 4–6 question or how-to H2s, each 2–4 sentence paragraphs and no H3s. A 5-question FAQ, three "Keep learning" related cards, and a closing CTA banner. |
| **Data post** | [GEO Toolbox — llms.txt](https://geotoolbox.ai/blog/llms-txt) | ~1,500–2,500 words | A "claim vs what the data shows vs source" table near the top. Our own numbers and screenshots, dated. Method and limits stated plainly. |
| **Verdict post** | [Optimizely — Does llms.txt work?](https://www.optimizely.com/field-notes/articles/does-llms-txt-work/) | ~1,200–1,800 words | A **Short version** box with 4–5 bullets. One table of studies. "Why", then "When it's still worth it", then "What to do instead" as numbered steps. |
| **Comparison** | Tool roundups that rank (HubSpot, Position Digital) | ~2,500–3,500 words | An "at a glance" table first, then one section per option with best-for, price and limits. Says how each was tested. |

Every style keeps the same house rules: the answer in the first screen, one proof element (a screenshot, number or example), an updated date, and one CTA tied to the Free AI Visibility Audit.
