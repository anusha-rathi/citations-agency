# Keyword research — Reddit traffic news post, 2 Nov 2026

Sources: US Google autocomplete (`gl=us`) and a read of who covers the story. No volume or difficulty data: Ahrefs returned "Insufficient plan" and Semrush is out of API units. DuckDuckGo's HTML endpoint returned no results, so no live SERP check was done.

## Finding 1 — target `reddit traffic decline`

US autocomplete returns `reddit traffic decline` and `reddit traffic is down`. Supporting terms: `reddit google deal`, `reddit google deal renewal`, `reddit google licensing deal`, `reddit google ai overview`, `reddit google ai mode`, `why does google ai use reddit`.

Those deal and AI terms return different search needs, but the post can cover them as sections for now. Split one out only if Search Console shows it pulling its own impressions.

## Finding 2 — the angle is what brands should do

Coverage found so far is news and investor-led: Search Engine Journal, CNBC, Yahoo Finance, TheStreet, Slashdot, PPC Land. None of what we read tells a Reddit marketer what to change. That's the thing our page has that theirs don't.

## Rejected — no click, or not ours

- `reddit stock down`, `reddit stock dropping today` — investor intent, finance sites will own it.
- `why does google ai use reddit` as the primary term — "why" queries have the highest AI Overview trigger rate (59.8%, Ahrefs, Sept 2025). Kept as a section only.

## Polluted terms — do not target

- `reddit traffic` alone returns `reddit traffic school`, `reddit traffic ticket`.
- `reddit google ai` alone returns Google AI Pro and AI Studio plan discussion.
